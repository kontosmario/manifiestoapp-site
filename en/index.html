What people ask first.

How much does it cost?

Manifiesto is a per-household subscription, not per person: Monthly Plan (US$4.99) or Annual Plan (US$39.99, the most chosen). You begin with 30 days of full access to get to know the whole app, and you cancel whenever you want. A single plan covers the whole household (up to 2 or 4 people depending on the plan). There are no half-features: you have the entire app from day one.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. Manifiesto doesn't connect to banks, wallets, or accounts. You enter expenses by hand (it's faster than it sounds) and you're in control of what gets logged and what doesn't.

How many people can share a household?

It depends on the plan: Monthly covers 2 people and Annual up to 4. It works the same whether you live alone or add someone: you set up the household however your situation works.

Is there an Android version?

v1 is iOS-only (iOS 17 or later). Android is coming in a later phase. If you want us to let you know when it's available, write to us.

What happens if I want to delete my account?

You go to Settings → Account → Delete account inside the app. Your account is scheduled to be deleted in 30 days (during that time you can cancel the deletion simply by signing back in). After 30 days, all the data is deleted irreversibly.

If I share the household, can they see how much I earn?

Each person enters the contribution they'll make for the month (not necessarily their entire income) and the app works on the total. If you'd rather share only the household total and not the individual breakdown, you can do that from Settings.
