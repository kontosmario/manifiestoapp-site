Delete your account

How to ask us to delete your Manifiesto account and its data, what gets deleted, what is kept and for how long.

01 In the app (iPhone and Android)

  1. Open Manifiesto and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Review what gets deleted and what is kept, and confirm.

If you own a shared space with other active members, the app will ask you to close the space first or wait for the others to leave.

02 Without the app, by email

If you no longer have the app, email soporte@manifiestoapp.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". To protect your account, we only act on requests sent from that address. We confirm within 5 business days.

03 What is deleted and when

  • 30 days to change your mind: the deletion is scheduled 30 days after you request it. During that time you can cancel it by signing in again.
  • After 30 days your profile, email, avatar, settings, cards, devices registered for notifications and your Mercado Pago connection, if any, are permanently deleted.

04 What is kept

  • Shared spaces: if the space has other active members, the expenses you added may stay visible to them, shown as "Anonymous", without your name or email.
  • Backups: database backups roll over within 30 days.
  • Legal obligations: we may keep what the law requires (for example, payment records for the tax period).

05 If you have a subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: the store where you bought it handles the billing. Cancel it first:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Android: Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
