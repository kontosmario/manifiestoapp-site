If you invite another person to your household, you represent that you have obtained their consent to share financial information with them. Manifiesto is not responsible for conflicts between members of the household.

If you leave a household of which you are a member but not the owner, you lose access to that household. The records you uploaded to the shared economy (for example, expenses) may remain visible to the other members.

If you are the owner and you delete the household, it is closed for all its members: the shared economy data is erased and we notify the other people so that they can continue with their own account.
