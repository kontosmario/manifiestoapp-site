02 What Manifiesto is

Manifiesto is a mobile application to organize your personal finances and those of your shared space. It lets you log expenses and income, see how much you can spend each day, keep track of your fixed expenses and installments, follow a savings goal and close each cycle with a recap. You can use it on your own or share a space with other people, for example your partner or your family.

Manifiesto is not:

  • A financial institution, bank or digital wallet.
  • A provider of financial, accounting, tax or legal advice.
  • A payment intermediary: it does not process payments or move money.
  • An investment platform.

Manifiesto is an organization tool: it does not connect to your bank accounts, cards, digital wallets or any other financial platform, and it does not access your banking information or anyone else's. Everything you see in the app is entered by you, in one of these ways:

  • By hand, in the app.
  • From a screenshot you choose to import, for example the transaction list from your bank or wallet app. The text in the screenshot is read on your own device, and you review each entry before it goes in.
  • With the Apple Pay shortcut, if you choose to use it. It is optional: you create it yourself in Apple's Shortcuts app and, when you pay contactless with Apple Pay, that shortcut passes the merchant and the amount of the payment to Manifiesto for you to confirm. Manifiesto does not access your Wallet or your cards.

That information is used only to help you organize and control your personal finances and those of your space.

05 Shared spaces

In Manifiesto your data lives in a space, which can be just yours or shared with other people. Each person belongs to one space at a time. Whatever any member logs is visible to everyone in the space.

Whoever creates the space is its "owner". Only the owner can invite other people, with a single-use code that expires after 7 days, and only the owner can remove them from the space.

How many people fit in a space depends on its plan (see Plans and full access): Plan Jardín is for one person and Plan Huerta allows up to four. While the owner's full access lasts, up to two people fit. Without a plan, the space does not add new members. If the plan ends or changes, nobody loses their place: only new invitations are stopped.

If you invite another person to your space, you represent that you have obtained their consent to share financial information with them. Manifiesto is not responsible for conflicts between the people in a space.

If you leave a space you do not own, or the owner removes you, you lose access to that space. What you logged (for example, your expenses) stays in the space and the other people can still see it. If the space's plan covered your access, it no longer does.

If you are the owner and you delete the space, or leave it, the space closes for everyone: its expenses, fixed expenses, goals and settings are deleted, and the other people see this when they open the app and can continue with their own account. This cannot be undone: coordinate with the people in your space before doing it. Manifiesto is not responsible for the resulting loss of access.

07 Plans and full access

Since version 2.1.0 of the app, Manifiesto works without a plan: a plan adds features and more room in your space. In version 2.0.1 and earlier, when full access ends the app asks for a plan to keep using it.

Without a plan you can:

  • Log your expenses for today and yesterday, and your income.
  • See what's left for today, your garden, your streak and the daily recap.
  • Use the Apple Pay shortcut and get reminders and alerts.
  • See the current cycle and the last closed edition.
  • Use fixed expenses, screenshot imports and the assistant, with limits. The app shows you each limit when you reach it.

A plan adds:

  • Fixed expenses with no limit.
  • Logging expenses, and marking calm days, on any past date.
  • Screenshot imports with no daily limit.
  • The full assistant.
  • All your past editions.
  • Each cycle's report as a PDF.
  • Creating your own categories and renaming the existing ones.
  • With Plan Huerta, inviting more people to your space, up to four in total.

The limits without a plan may change in new versions of the app. A limit never deletes what you already logged: if you have more fixed expenses than the limit, you keep them and just can't add more; and your past editions stay saved even if you can't open them without a plan.

Full access to start. When you create your account you get full access for a while, with no purchase and no card. There is one per account, it starts the day you create it, and the app shows you how many days you have left. It is not an Apple introductory offer: there is no purchase, nothing is charged when it ends and no subscription starts. The app keeps working with everything that doesn't need a plan, and what you logged stays saved.

08 Subscriptions and payments

Plans are auto-renewable subscriptions purchased inside the app, through Apple's App Store. Since version 2.1.0 there are two plans, each monthly or yearly:

  • Plan Jardín, for a one-person space: US$2.99 a month or US$24.99 a year.
  • Plan Huerta, for a shared space of up to four people: US$4.99 a month or US$41.99 a year.

Those are the prices on the United States App Store. In your country, the App Store shows you the local price before you confirm. Version 2.0.1 and earlier offer the Monthly Plan and the Annual Plan; since 2.1.0 they are no longer sold, and anyone who has one keeps it, with the same features and room as Plan Huerta.

For all plans:

  • Purchase: it is made inside the app and processed by Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Manifiesto does not process payments or see your card details.
  • One plan per space: the subscription is linked to the space you purchased it from and covers everyone in it. Only the person who purchased it can change or cancel it.
  • Automatic renewal: the subscription renews automatically, at the same price, at the end of each period, unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the period ends. The renewal charge happens within those 24 hours.
  • Cancellation: you can cancel it anytime from your Apple Account settings, or in the app from Settings → Your plan → Plan for your space → Manage or cancel in App Store. You keep the plan until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Changing plans: upgrading takes effect immediately; downgrading takes effect at your next renewal.
  • Refunds: Apple decides them, according to its policies, and you request them at reportaproblem.apple.com. Manifiesto cannot issue refunds.
  • Price changes: if we change a plan's price, Apple notifies you before it applies and, when its rules require it, asks you to accept it. A price change never applies to a period you already paid for.

Purchases are also governed by the Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions.
